Institutional Establishment Support

Establishing a new university or college in India typically takes 18 to 36 months, from feasibility to admission-ready approval. UniMarg acts as the promoter's project office across the entire pathway: feasibility, financial modelling, statutory approval, inspection and institutional readiness, so one accountable team carries the project from vision to the first academic session.

Overview

From concept to campus, one accountable team.

UniMarg partners with promoters, trusts, corporates and educational organisations to establish universities, colleges, schools, research institutions and skill universities. We carry the project from vision to the first academic session, so the financial case, the statutory application and the inspection are handled by a single team rather than a chain of disconnected consultants.

Our Approach

Approvals earned through preparation, not last-minute effort.

We treat establishment as a managed programme with defined milestones, so committee presentations and inspection days are rehearsed events rather than one-off crises.

How we work

  • Committee presentations and inspection days run as rehearsed, prepared events
  • A single project office accountable to the promoter for milestones, documentation and query resolution

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How long does it take to establish a new university or college in India?

Timelines vary by category and state, typically 18 to 36 months from feasibility to admission-ready approval. UniMarg builds a milestone schedule against the specific regulatory pathway at the outset of the engagement.

Do you only prepare documentation, or also manage the inspection itself?

Both. UniMarg's Project Management Support covers the full inspection cycle, preparing the institution's team, managing the committee visit, and resolving queries raised on the day and afterward.

Can you take over a stalled establishment project that is already in progress?

Yes. A number of engagements begin mid-process, where UniMarg audits the existing application, identifies gaps against current regulatory requirements, and rebuilds the project plan from that point forward.

Can a company or corporate group establish a university or college in India?

Yes, subject to the promoter and structuring norms of the relevant State Act or regulatory body. UniMarg advises corporate promoters on the correct legal and institutional structure before any application is filed.
