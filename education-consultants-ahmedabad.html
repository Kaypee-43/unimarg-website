Why Local Matters

Why an Ahmedabad-based partner.

Gujarat has one of the country's most active higher education landscapes, from private universities established under State Acts to skill universities and legacy institutes. A consultancy on the ground understands the State Government pathways, moves quickly for committee visits and inspections, and meets promoters in person when a decision needs the board table, not an email thread.

What proximity gives you

  • On-site presence for committee presentations and regulatory inspection days
  • Familiarity with Gujarat State Government and university affiliation pathways
  • In-person promoter and board engagement for strategic decisions
  • Faster turnaround on documentation and query resolution cycles
  • A national advisory, accreditation and admissions practice, delivered from a Gujarat base
