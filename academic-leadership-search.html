Academic & Leadership Search

UniMarg places leadership and academic talent, from Vice Chancellors, Registrars and Deans to faculty and admission teams. Every candidate is drawn from an extensive database of education-sector professionals and screened against both the role brief and statutory qualification norms, so shortlisted candidates meet UGC and AICTE eligibility as well as institutional fit.

Schedule a consultation All seven practices

Overview

Institutions are built by people.

We find the people who can build them. UniMarg searches against both the role brief and statutory qualification norms, screens for governance and culture fit rather than credentials alone, and presents a shortlisted, pre-vetted panel rather than an open pipeline.

Pain Points

What institutions ask us.

We cannot find a Vice Chancellor who meets UGC eligibility norms.

Our Registrar resigned mid-year and we need an urgent, qualified replacement.

We are short on faculty right before an AICTE or UGC inspection.

We need a full leadership team in place before our institution can even apply for approval.

We do not have a proper recruitment process, induction structure or training framework for new staff.

Our Approach

Statutory fit and culture fit, together.

Regulatory eligibility and institutional culture are treated as core search criteria from the outset, not add-ons discovered late.

How we work

  • Search against both the role brief and statutory qualification norms (UGC, AICTE and relevant councils)
  • Candidates screened against institutional culture and governance fit, not credentials alone
  • A shortlisted, pre-vetted panel presented rather than an open pipeline
  • Onboarding supported through the candidate’s first academic cycle where required

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can you recruit for roles with mandatory statutory qualifications, like Vice Chancellor or Registrar?

Yes. UGC, AICTE and relevant statutory council qualification norms are built into the search brief from the outset, so shortlisted candidates meet regulatory eligibility as well as institutional fit.

Do you handle both leadership search and bulk faculty hiring?

Yes. The practice covers single senior leadership searches such as VC, Registrar and Dean, as well as multi-position faculty and administrative hiring for a new or expanding institution.

How is this different from a general HR consultancy?

This is a sector-specific search practice: statutory qualification norms, higher-education governance structures and institutional culture are treated as core search criteria, not add-ons.
