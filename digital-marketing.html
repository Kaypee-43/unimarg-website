Overview

Where Market Outreach & Admissions is a cyclical, admissions-driven funnel, this practice is ongoing brand-building: website, content, social presence and perception management that grows in value over time. Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How is Digital Marketing Support different from Market Outreach & Admissions?

Digital Marketing Support is ongoing brand and reputation work independent of any intake cycle. Market Outreach & Admissions is the admissions funnel built on top of that brand foundation.

Do you build and manage the institution’s website directly?

UniMarg defines the content architecture, messaging and priority pages; website build and ongoing management are delivered directly or coordinated with the institution’s technical partner, depending on scope.

What is AI-discoverability and why does it matter for an institution?

As prospective students and parents increasingly research institutions through AI-powered search and answer tools, a well-structured, comprehensive digital presence is more likely to be surfaced accurately in those results. This practice is built with that in mind.

We are planning a rebrand. What does that process actually involve?

A rebrand typically covers positioning research, visual identity, messaging and a phased rollout across the website, prospectus, signage and digital channels. UniMarg scopes this as a structured project rather than a logo change.
