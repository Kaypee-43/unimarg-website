About UniMarg

The name is the promise.
Uni + Marg: the path to great institutions.

"Marg" means path. UniMarg is a boutique consultancy firm and a trusted project management organisation in the Higher Education sector in India. We deliver expertise across University/Institute Establishment, Market Outreach, Students Recruitment, Statutory Support, Advisory Support and Recruitment Services.

Who We Are

End-to-end, from idea to implementation.

UniMarg undertakes end-to-end projects on a retainer basis, from idea to implementation. We ensure excellent management of activities: from identification of objectives through continuous monitoring, leading to the best achievement of objectives within the stipulated time frame.

We stay deliberately boutique. A small senior team, a limited number of active mandates, and direct founder involvement in every engagement. Our clients get partners who know their project intimately, not a rotating bench of analysts.

02

Honest counsel
