Overview

One funnel, one accountable partner.

Outreach and admissions are not two projects; they are two ends of one institutional growth journey. UniMarg maps current positioning against target positioning, identifies the audiences and opinion leaders that matter, and builds the CRM, lead management, counselling and calling systems that turn reach into enrolled students.

Our Approach

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you run digital campaigns yourselves, or only build the strategy?

UniMarg designs the outreach and admission strategy, builds the campaign infrastructure and coordinates execution, working alongside the institution’s marketing team or vendors as needed.

How is Market Outreach & Admissions different from Digital Marketing Support?

Market Outreach & Admissions is the admissions funnel, positioning through enrolment, run on a recruitment cycle. Digital Marketing Support is the institution’s ongoing brand, reputation and digital presence, independent of any single intake.

Can you work with our existing admissions team rather than replacing it?

Yes. Most engagements involve enabling and instrumenting an existing admissions team rather than replacing it, through CRM, workflows, scripts and dashboards.

Why are our admission numbers falling even though we advertise every year?

Advertising spend without a positioning strategy, segmented value propositions and a managed counselling funnel typically produces enquiries that never convert. The fix is usually the funnel, not the ad budget.
