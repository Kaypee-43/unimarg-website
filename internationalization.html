Internationalization

UniMarg identifies the right international partners and structures agreements, dual degrees, twinning programmes, exchanges and academic collaborations, that meet UGC’s foreign collaboration norms and deliver real value to students and faculty. We also set up the international office capability so partnerships are managed, not just signed.

Overview

Partnerships that hold up in practice.

The practice identifies partners aligned to the client’s academic profile, structures agreements for operational follow-through, and builds UGC foreign-collaboration compliance into every agreement from the outset. It also covers international student recruitment and the infrastructure to support it.

Our Approach

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you help find international partner institutions, or only structure existing relationships?

Both. UniMarg identifies and approaches suitable partner institutions where none yet exist, and structures and manages agreements where a relationship is already underway.

Is international student recruitment part of this practice or the general admissions practice?

International admissions sits within Internationalization, since the regulatory, cultural and marketing considerations for international students differ materially from the domestic admissions funnel.

How do you ensure UGC compliance on foreign collaborations?

Compliance with UGC’s foreign collaboration regulations is built into MoU drafting and structuring from the outset, rather than reviewed after an agreement is signed.
