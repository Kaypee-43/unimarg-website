Questions

Frequently asked

How long does it take to establish a new university or college in India?

Timelines vary by category and state, and are indicative until confirmed. As a broad guide it is often in the range of 18 to 36 months from feasibility to admission-ready approval. UniMarg builds a milestone schedule against your specific regulatory pathway at the outset.

Can you take over a project that is already in progress?

Yes. Many engagements begin mid-process. UniMarg audits the existing application or plan, identifies gaps against current requirements, and rebuilds from that point forward.

Do you only advise, or do you also execute?

Both. Engagements run in one of three modes, advisory, execution, or milestone-linked delivery, matched to what your institution needs, with accountability stated clearly up front.

Can a company or corporate group establish an institution?

Yes, subject to the promoter and structuring norms of the relevant State Act or regulatory body. UniMarg advises on the correct legal and institutional structure before any application is filed.

Who typically engages UniMarg?

Engagements are usually led directly with Chairmen, Trustees, Vice Chancellors and promoters, given the governance and strategic nature of the work.
