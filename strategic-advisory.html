Strategic Advisory Support

UniMarg’s Strategic Advisory practice sits at the board table, not the project level. We support governance, growth and institutional transformation, from five-year development plans and new programme business cases to financial sustainability, fundraising strategy and multi-institute performance visibility, as continuous counsel rather than a one-time report.

Overview

A partner at the board table.

This is stage-wise counsel across an institution’s full lifecycle, not a single deliverable. UniMarg engages at board and promoter level, benchmarks the institution against comparable ones, and builds financial sustainability and fundraising strategy alongside academic growth plans rather than after them.

Pain Points

What institutions ask us.

Our enrolment has plateaued and the board is asking what our growth strategy actually is.

We need to raise funds for a new building, lab or research centre.

Our board wants a five-year institutional development plan.

Should we launch new programmes, and which ones make sense for us?

We run several institutes under one university and leadership has no consolidated view of performance.

We are eligible for research grants but do not have the bandwidth to identify or apply for them.

Our Approach

Counsel that stays at the table.

We engage where the decisions are actually made, and stay through implementation rather than handing over a report and leaving.

How we work

  • Engagement at board and promoter level, not only at the operational level
  • The institution benchmarked against comparable institutions before any direction is recommended
  • Financial sustainability and fundraising strategy built alongside academic growth plans, not after them
  • Continuous, retainer-based counsel rather than a one-time report

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is Strategic Advisory a one-time engagement or an ongoing retainer?

Most Strategic Advisory relationships run as a retainer, since governance and growth decisions are continuous rather than one-off, though a bounded diagnostic engagement is also available.

Does Strategic Advisory include fundraising execution, or only strategy?

UniMarg builds the fundraising strategy, case-for-support and prospect research; execution support such as donor outreach, CSR partnerships and grant applications is scoped as part of the same retainer.

Who within the institution typically engages UniMarg for Strategic Advisory?

Engagements are usually led directly with the Chairman, Trustees, Vice Chancellor or promoter, given the governance and strategic nature of the work.

How do I choose which new academic programmes to launch?

UniMarg builds a business case for each candidate programme covering market demand, regulatory eligibility, competitive positioning and financial viability, so the decision is evidence-led rather than a guess.
