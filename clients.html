How Engagements Run

One retainer. The entire path.

Engagements with our client institutions draw on ten practice areas, delivered either as one integrated retainer or as focused mandates: establishment, statutory support, student recruitment, market outreach, advisory support, talent recruitment, NEP 2020 implementation, international partnerships, accreditation and ranking, and fundraising strategy.

Because the team is small and senior, a co-founder stays directly involved in every engagement. Institutions work with the people who designed the plan, not a rotating delivery bench.
