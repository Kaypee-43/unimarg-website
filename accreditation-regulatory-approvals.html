Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do you help with first-time NAAC accreditation as well as re-accreditation cycles?

Yes. UniMarg supports institutions through both first cycle and subsequent accreditation cycles, including SSR preparation, AQAR filing and peer team readiness.

Can you handle AICTE Extension of Approval on an annual basis?

Yes. Extension of Approval is a recurring, time-bound filing UniMarg manages directly, including documentation and query resolution with the regulator.

How does NEP 2020 implementation connect to accreditation?

NAAC, NBA and NIRF frameworks increasingly assess curriculum flexibility, credit structures and multiple entry-exit provisions, so NEP alignment and accreditation readiness are addressed together, not as separate projects.

What is the difference between UGC Recognition and AICTE Approval?

UGC Recognition establishes a university’s legal standing to award degrees; AICTE Approval governs technical education programmes specifically. Many institutions need both, and UniMarg maps exactly which approvals apply before filing anything.
