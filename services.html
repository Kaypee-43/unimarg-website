Our Services

Ten practices.
One accountable partner.

UniMarg undertakes end-to-end projects on a retainer basis, from idea to implementation, with continuous monitoring so objectives are achieved within the stipulated time frame. Engage us for the full journey or for a focused mandate across ten practice areas.

01 · Establish

University / Institute Establishment

We manage the complete establishment process for universities and institutes. From the first financial model to the final inspection, we act as the promoter's project office, converting vision into an approved, admission-ready institution.

We also provide Project Management Support (PMS) for handling UGC and AICTE inspection visits, so your team faces committees prepared and confident.

What we deliver

  • Effective financial models and Detailed Project Reports (DPRs)
  • Application development for regulatory bodies, per prescribed standards
  • Committee presentations and physical inspection management
  • Resolution of queries raised by committees
  • Project Management Support for UGC and AICTE inspection visits

02 · Reach

Market Outreach

We develop branding and market outreach strategies by mapping the institute's current positioning against where it needs to be, in line with current and future academic launch plans.

Before any campaign begins, we invest time in understanding the values, ethos and culture of the institution, so the target audience, opinion leaders and institution builders we reach are the right ones.

What we deliver

  • Institutional positioning mapping and analysis
  • Branding and market outreach campaign strategy
  • Audience, opinion leader and stakeholder identification
  • Outreach aligned to academic launch plans
  • Digital marketing coordination and campaign infrastructure

03 · Recruit

Students Recruitment

Student enrolment is one of the biggest challenges institutions face today. UniMarg specialises in developing and delivering marketing strategies that direct the institute's resources more effectively toward the right audience.

We identify the highest-priority student segments and define value propositions tailored to each, then build the systems, CRM, lead management, counselling workflows and AI-assisted calling, that convert interest into enrolment.

What we deliver

  • Admission strategy, segment prioritisation and value propositions
  • CRM integration and lead management workflows
  • AI calling and counselling frameworks
  • Landing pages and performance-driven campaigns
  • Admission team enablement and funnel dashboards

04 · Comply

Statutory Support

UniMarg specialises in university enactment and the establishment of institutions in accordance with State, UGC and relevant council regulations. We turn compliance from a recurring crisis into a planned, managed workstream.

What we deliver

  • Detailed Feasibility Reports (DFRs) and Detailed Project Reports (DPRs)
  • Applications for statutory and regulatory approvals
  • Advice on gathering and submitting statutory documentation
  • State act, UGC and council compliance support
  • Inspection readiness and liaison with regulatory agencies

05 · Advise

Advisory Support

We offer project advisory support at every stage of an institution's establishment. We understand brand-building and institutional development processes, and provide strategic consulting for interactions with statutory and regulatory agencies, inspections, presentations and collaborative relationships.

What we deliver

  • Stage-wise advisory across the establishment lifecycle
  • Strategic support for regulatory interactions and inspections
  • Presentation and committee-facing preparation
  • Partnership and collaboration structuring
  • Institutional development and brand-building counsel

06 · Staff

Recruitment Services

Institutions are built by people. Our experienced recruiters specialise in finding the best candidates for a wide variety of roles in the education sector, drawing on an extensive database of qualified candidates.

A rigorous screening process ensures that only the most suitable applicants are presented to our clients.

What we deliver

  • Leadership search: Vice Chancellors, Directors, Deans, Registrars
  • Faculty recruitment aligned to statutory norms
  • Administrative and admission team hiring
  • Rigorous candidate screening and shortlisting
  • Access to an extensive database of qualified candidates

07 · NEP 2020

NEP 2020 Implementation

The National Education Policy 2020 asks institutions to rethink curriculum structure, credit systems, and academic flexibility. We translate its mandates into practical, implementable frameworks rather than compliance paperwork.

What we deliver

  • Multidisciplinary and credit-based curriculum redesign
  • Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) and multiple entry-exit planning
  • Faculty orientation on NEP-aligned pedagogy
  • Institutional Development Plan alignment with NEP goals
  • Compliance documentation for state and UGC review

08 · Global

International Partnerships

Global collaborations raise an institution's academic standing and its appeal to students seeking international exposure. We identify the right partners and structure agreements that hold up in practice, not just on paper.

What we deliver

  • Partner institution identification and outreach
  • MoU drafting and negotiation support
  • Dual-degree and exchange programme structuring
  • Compliance with UGC regulations on foreign collaborations
  • Ongoing partnership management and renewal support

09 · Rank

Accreditation & Ranking

NAAC, NBA and NIRF outcomes influence everything from funding eligibility to student trust. We guide institutions through the full cycle: self-study preparation, documentation, and the peer team visit itself.

What we deliver

  • NAAC Self Study Report (SSR) preparation
  • NBA accreditation documentation for individual programmes
  • NIRF data compilation and ranking improvement strategy
  • Peer team visit preparation and mock audits
  • IQAC setup and quality assurance framework design

10 · Fund

Fundraising Strategy

Infrastructure, research, and scholarship programmes all need funding beyond tuition. We help institutions build a credible case for support and pursue the right mix of government, corporate, and philanthropic sources.

What we deliver

  • Fundraising strategy and case-for-support development
  • Government grant identification and application support
  • CSR partnership facilitation
  • Donor and prospect research
  • Fundraising campaign planning and execution support

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What does UniMarg do?

UniMarg is a boutique project management and consulting organisation working in the higher education sector in India. It undertakes end-to-end projects on a retainer basis, from idea to implementation, covering institution establishment, approvals, outreach and admissions.

Which services does UniMarg offer?

UniMarg works across ten practice areas. The six core services are University/Institute Establishment, Market Outreach, Students Recruitment, Statutory Support, Advisory Support and Recruitment Services. Four specialised services cover NEP 2020 Implementation, International Partnerships, Accreditation and Ranking, and Fundraising Strategy.

Does UniMarg help establish a new university from scratch?

Yes. UniMarg manages the complete establishment process for universities and institutes, acting as the promoter's project office. This covers financial models and Detailed Project Reports, applications to regulatory bodies, committee presentations, physical inspection management and resolution of committee queries.

What is Project Management Support for UGC and AICTE inspections?

Project Management Support, or PMS, is UniMarg's hands-on preparation and coordination for UGC and AICTE inspection visits. The aim is that the institution's team faces inspection committees prepared, with documentation, presentations and query responses ready.

Does UniMarg support NEP 2020 implementation?

Yes. NEP 2020 Implementation is one of UniMarg's specialised practice areas, providing advisory and implementation support for institutions aligning their structure, programmes and academic delivery with the National Education Policy 2020.

How do UniMarg engagements work?

UniMarg undertakes projects on a retainer basis. Clients can engage the firm for the full journey from idea to implementation, or for a focused mandate in a single practice area. The firm stays deliberately boutique, with a small senior team and direct founder involvement in every engagement.

Where is UniMarg located?

UniMarg is based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, at 812 Ganesh Glory, Jagatpur Road, off the Sarkhej-Gandhinagar Highway, Jagatpur, 382481. The firm works with institutions across India.
