Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What does UniMarg do?

UniMarg is a boutique project management and consulting organisation working in the higher education sector in India. It undertakes end-to-end projects on a retainer basis, from idea to implementation, covering institution establishment, approvals, outreach and admissions.

Which services does UniMarg offer?

UniMarg works across seven advisory practices: Institutional Establishment Support, Market Outreach and Admissions, Recognition, Accreditation and Regulatory Approvals, Strategic Advisory Support, Academic and Leadership Search, Digital Marketing Support, and Internationalization.

Does UniMarg help establish a new university from scratch?

Yes. UniMarg manages the complete establishment process for universities and institutes, acting as the promoter's project office. This covers financial models and Detailed Project Reports, applications to regulatory bodies, committee presentations, physical inspection management and resolution of committee queries.

What is Project Management Support for UGC and AICTE inspections?

Project Management Support, or PMS, is UniMarg's hands-on preparation and coordination for UGC and AICTE inspection visits. The aim is that the institution's team faces inspection committees prepared, with documentation, presentations and query responses ready.

Does UniMarg support NEP 2020 implementation?

Yes. NEP 2020 alignment sits within UniMarg's Recognition, Accreditation and Regulatory Approvals practice. It covers multidisciplinary and credit-based curriculum redesign, the Academic Bank of Credits and multiple entry-exit planning, addressed together with NAAC, NBA and NIRF readiness rather than as a separate project.

How do UniMarg engagements work?

UniMarg undertakes projects on a retainer basis. Clients can engage the firm for the full journey from idea to implementation, or for a focused mandate in a single practice area. The firm stays deliberately boutique, with a small senior team and direct founder involvement in every engagement.

Where is UniMarg located?

UniMarg is based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, at 812 Ganesh Glory, Jagatpur Road, off the Sarkhej-Gandhinagar Highway, Jagatpur, 382481. The firm works with institutions across India.
